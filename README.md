# 🛍️ Smart Retail Analytics Suite

### A 4-dashboard business intelligence solution for a UAE retail chain
**Customers · Products · Stores · Transactions**

</div>

---

## 📌 Overview

This project turns raw retail data from Dubai and the wider UAE into **four interactive dashboards** that answer the questions a retail leadership team actually asks:

- **Who are our customers, and who is about to leave?**
- **What are we selling, and what is about to run out?**
- **Which stores and areas drive footfall?**
- **How do customers pay, and through which channel?**

Every dashboard shares one visual language (teal and gold on cream) and includes slicers for fast filtering, KPI cards for the headline numbers, and short written insights next to the charts.

## 📊 Dashboards at a Glance

### 1️⃣ Smart Retail Customer Dashboard
Understand customer demographics, loyalty and churn risk.

| KPI | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Customers | **1.5K** |
| Churn Risk Score | **740.11** |
| Loyalty Points | **19M** |
| Avg Monthly Salary | **AED 901.03K** |

**Visuals:** Age vs Risk Score scatter · Churn Risk vs Loyalty Tier · Customers by Gender · New Customer Joining trend
**Filters:** Age, Gender, App Installed, Marketing opt-in

**Key insights**
- 🥉 **Bronze-tier customers carry the highest churn risk**, almost 3x Platinum. Moving customers up a loyalty tier is a stronger lever than retention outreach alone.
- ⚖️ The gender split is almost perfectly even (about 50/50), so campaigns can stay broadly targeted.
- 📉 New customer acquisition peaked around 2024 and has been falling since, with 2026 the lowest point in the series.

---

### 2️⃣ Retail Product Dashboard
Track stock health, inventory value and reorder needs across the catalogue.

| KPI | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Products | **400** |
| Total Stock | **103K units** |
| Products to Reorder | **24** |
| Inventory Value | **AED 104.00M** |

**Visuals:** Reorder watch-list table · Stock by Brand treemap · In Stock vs Reorder donut · Net Sales by Category · Stock by Category
**Filters:** Product category

**Key insights**
- ✅ **94% of products are in stock**, with 6% (24 SKUs) at or below reorder level.
- 💎 **Jewellery & Watches and Home & Furniture lead net sales**, followed by Sporting Goods and Electronics.
- 📦 Stock is spread fairly evenly across categories (about 7K to 11K units each).

---

### 3️⃣ Retail Stores Dashboard
Compare store footprint, foot traffic and IoT sensor coverage across Dubai.

| KPI | Value |
|---|---|
| Avg Daily Footfall | **89K** |
| Total Store Size | **124K sq ft** |
| IoT Sensors | **884** |

**Visuals:** IoT Sensor Count vs Daily Footfall · Brands by Category · Brands by Area · Store Size by Year
**Filters:** Month, Area (Al Barsha, Dubai Marina, Deira, Palm Jumeirah, Downtown Dubai, Jebel Ali and more)

**Key insights**
- 🗺️ **Deira has the largest brand presence (about 15%)**, followed by Al Wasl, Jebel Ali and Sheikh Zayed Road.
- 🏪 Home & Furniture has the most brands of any category.
- 📈 Store expansion peaked in two waves, around 2018 and 2022.

---

### 4️⃣ Retail Transaction Dashboard
Analyse revenue, discounts and payment behaviour across channels.

| KPI | Value |
|---|---|
| Gross Amount | **AED 23.44M** |
| Discount Amount | **AED 1.40M** |
| Net Amount | **AED 22.04M** |
| Quantity Sold | **15K** |
| Loyalty Points Earned | **2M** |

**Visuals:** Subcategory count by Payment Method and Channel · Net Amount by Payment Method · Financial metrics by Customer Satisfaction · Quantity share by Payment Method
**Filters:** Month

**Key insights**
- 💳 **Credit cards lead** in both sales value and volume (about 28% of units), followed by Debit Card and Apple Pay.
- 🏬 **In-store is the dominant channel** across every payment method.
- 📲 Digital wallets (Apple Pay, Samsung Pay, Careem Pay) and BNPL options (Tabby, Postpay) make up a meaningful share of payments.
- 🏷️ Discounts are about **6% of gross sales**.
